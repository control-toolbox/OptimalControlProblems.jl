Status ​

The status of each problem in each modeling, and the latest convergence report of each cron. A problem is valid in a modeling once it is checked there, draft before; a draft is documented but left out of problems(backend). Convergence is reported, never tested: a solve that does not converge, or that reaches another solution than the best known one, is listed below and does not fail anything.
